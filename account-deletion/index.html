Account control

Delete your Aikyra account

Last updated: 18 August 2026

You can permanently delete your Aikyra account directly from the app. For security, Aikyra requires a recent sign-in before completing deletion.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Aikyra and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Under Account deletion, select Delete account.
  4. Choose whether eligible Aikyra data stored locally on this device should be kept or erased.
  5. Verify your identity using the supported sign-in method.
  6. Select Permanently delete account or Finish deleting account and confirm.

What is deleted

Deletion permanently removes the Aikyra account and Aikyra-controlled server data associated with it, including:

  • account profile and authentication link;
  • synchronized health and wellness records;
  • registered Aikyra devices and sync state;
  • Family Care connections, permissions and Aikyra-controlled shared content;
  • stored Aikyra product-analytics counts, if previously enabled; and
  • Aikyra purchase entitlement records, subject to transaction records that Google Play or law requires to be retained.

Local and third-party data

During deletion, you choose whether to keep eligible local Aikyra records for guest use or erase them from that device. Data controlled by Health Connect, Apple Health, system Calendar, Google Play or another independent provider is not deleted from that provider merely by deleting Aikyra. Use the provider's own controls where applicable.

If you cannot access the app

Email gauravpatil5152@gmail.com from the address associated with your Aikyra account and write “Aikyra account deletion” in the subject. Do not send passwords, verification codes, purchase tokens or health records by email. We will provide a secure identity-verification path before acting.

Deletion cannot be undone. Export any eligible records you want to keep before permanently deleting the account.
