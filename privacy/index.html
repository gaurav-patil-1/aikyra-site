Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aikyra, operated by Gaurav Patil ("Aikyra", "we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you use the Aikyra mobile application and related services. Aikyra is a health and wellness organisation tool. It is not an emergency service or a substitute for professional medical care.

1. Information we handle

Account information

If you create an account, we process identifiers such as your email address, authentication provider identifier, display name, session information and registered-device metadata. Passwords and authentication credentials are handled by our authentication provider; Aikyra does not store your plain-text password.

Health and wellness records

Aikyra can store records you choose to enter, including medication schedules and dose history, hydration, habits, sleep, activity, meals and nutrition, symptoms, mood and self-recorded stress, vitals, laboratory facts, appointments, mindfulness activity and related notes. If supported health-data sync is enabled for your signed-in account, eligible records are sent securely to Aikyra's hosted service so they can remain available across your devices.

Private Journal and Cycle data have separate sync controls. They remain on your device unless you separately enable their account sync. Certain imported provider records and device context remain device-only as described in the app. We do not upload user-generated photos, audio, video, prescription images or other health media.

Family Care information

If you use Family Care, we process invitations, relationship and permission settings, sharing status, accountability choices and the specific supported records you authorize another person to view. Family Care does not silently share all account data.

Purchases

Google Play processes payment details. Aikyra receives purchase identifiers, product and plan information, entitlement state and lifecycle timestamps needed to verify, restore and manage access. We do not receive your full card number.

Optional product analytics

Product analytics are off by default. If you opt in, Aikyra records only bounded, account-linked daily counts for opening the app and opening Settings & Help. We do not include health records, notes, search text or advertising identifiers in these counts. Turning the setting off stops collection and requests deletion of the stored counts.

Device permissions and local information

With your permission, Aikyra may interact with Android Health Connect or Apple Health, notifications, Bluetooth health devices, system calendar, system voice input and approved location automations. The app explains the applicable boundary before access. Approved-place coordinates stay encrypted on the device and are not uploaded. Calendar access is limited to the features you choose; private notes and medication details are not written to calendar events.

2. How we use information

We use information only as needed to:

  • provide the records, reminders, sync, exports, insights and Family Care functions you request;
  • authenticate accounts and protect account boundaries;
  • verify purchases and apply subscription status;
  • respond to support, privacy and account-deletion requests;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse and diagnose service reliability; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.
No health-data advertising. We do not sell personal or health information, and we do not use health records for targeted advertising.

3. Services that process information

Depending on the features you choose, information may be processed by:

  • Supabase, for authentication, hosted PostgreSQL account sync, server functions and real-time change signals;
  • Google Identity or Sign in with Apple, when you choose that authentication method;
  • Google Play, for Android purchases, verification and subscription lifecycle events;
  • Brevo, only when a Family Care invitation email is requested and email delivery is available;
  • Open Food Facts, for a user-requested packaged-food lookup without an Aikyra account identifier; and
  • U.S. National Library of Medicine RxNorm services, for a user-requested medication lookup without an Aikyra account identifier.

Android Health Connect, Apple Health and system Calendar operate as device-platform services. Their own privacy terms also apply. We require service providers acting for Aikyra to process information only for authorized purposes and under appropriate safeguards.

4. Storage and security

Aikyra protects local records using platform-protected encrypted storage where supported and sends hosted data over encrypted network connections. Hosted services need technical access to process synchronized records, so account sync is not end-to-end encrypted. We use access controls, row-level authorization, server-side purchase verification and account-bound data access. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

5. Retention and deletion

Hosted account information and synchronized records are retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, resolve security or billing matters, and meet legal obligations. Device-only records remain until you remove them, remove the app data or use an applicable in-app removal control. Providers such as Google Play may retain their own transaction records under their policies and legal obligations.

You can permanently delete your Aikyra account from Account → Account deletion → Delete account. After recent identity verification, deletion removes the account and server-held Aikyra data, including synchronized records, registered devices and Family Care connections. You separately choose whether to keep or erase eligible local data on that device. See the account-deletion instructions.

6. Your choices and rights

You can use core parts of Aikyra without an account, control supported health, Journal and Cycle sync, revoke device permissions, disconnect supported providers, export eligible records, manage Family Care access, disable optional analytics and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, object to, port or erase personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

7. Children

Aikyra is not directed to children for independent account creation. An adult account holder may use specifically identified managed-profile features only where they have appropriate parental, guardian or care authority. Do not submit a child's information without the authority and consent required by applicable law.

8. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use contractual, technical and organisational safeguards for international processing. The available privacy rights may vary by location.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Aikyra changes. We will update the date above and provide additional notice where required. Material changes will not silently expand health-data use beyond the choices described in the app.
